# Agriculture Data Portal — colleague review

Please look at the Western Visayas portal and answer these questions. If a figure looks wrong, note the section, commodity/group, year or period, place, and the value shown so we can trace it to the source file.

1. **Which three questions should the portal answer first?** For example: Which province produces the most palay? How many RSBSA rice farmers are registered? How much rice area is verified?
2. **Which units or values look wrong?** Choose any: vegetables/root crops (metric tons or hectares); livestock (metric tons, liveweight); poultry (metric tons, liveweight or egg weight); other crops (metric tons); RSBSA people; RSBSA land (hectares or percent). Give one example.
3. **Which RSBSA groups matter most to your work?** Choose up to four: all registrants, farmers, farm workers, fisherfolk, agri-youth, rice farmers, corn farmers, HVCDP farmers, poultry farmers, livestock farmers.
4. **Which RSBSA indicators should be prominent?** Choose up to four: total, gender, average age, seniors, IP, PWD, 4Ps, ARB, physical area, declared area, verified area, verification rate.
5. **What geographic comparison is most useful?** Western Visayas trend; province comparison; municipality detail; all three. Are there specific places you need to find quickly?
6. **Which chart makes the data easiest to understand?** Trend line over time; ranked bars by place; side-by-side rice/corn area bars; profile comparison across groups; a detailed table. Which chart is confusing?
7. **How should we handle historical regional boundary changes and partial 2026 data?** Show a visible break and note; hide cross-boundary comparisons; or another approach?
8. **What decision or action would you make using this portal?** What one missing figure or explanation prevents that today?

Optional: Please send a screenshot or source row for any discrepancy. Avoid sharing names or personal records; the portal works with aggregate data.
